## 1. Purpose
- A **claim** is the distributor's request to the company to reimburse the promotion discounts it gave to outlets on delivered orders; claims for promotions and damages are **settled outside DCODE**, based on a claims generation report [stated 2026-10-07 doc:Promotions and Budgets 2026_R2.pptx s.42-50] [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.10].
- DCODE's part: a **claim job** builds the claims from delivered orders of qualifying promotions; the result is read on **Claim Inquiry** [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.2-7].
- Place in the business: after the Daily Cycle. A claim needs orders already **Delivered** (Cashmemo Status, group 11 seq 33) and is built from the discount read on Transaction Inquiry > Total Offering [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.8, s.11]. Not part of framework group 11.

## 5. Process: the business steps in order
1. [Maker] Book and deliver orders with a claimable promotion (Promotion Layout Claim = Yes, Claimable % set; resultant Bonus Type 2 or Trade Offer); the cash memos must be Delivered [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.8, s.10].
2. [System / Maker] On the next day (N), run the claim job: automatically per its schedule, or manually on Adhoc Job Executor (select the job details, Click Execute) -> "Message sent successfully." [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.6-7]. The message only says the request was sent; it does **not** prove a claim was created [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.7].
3. [Maker] Navigate to Claim Inquiry; find the claim (Last Execution Date = the job run); open Claim Detail (promotions) and Reference Info (outlets, invoices) [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.2-5].
4. [Maker] Compare each claim amount with Claimable % x the promotion discount on Transaction Inquiry > Total Offering of the orders [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.11].
5. [System] Claim data goes to **Atlas** (DIST_CD, Claim_No, Cust_CD, Doc_No, Dist_claim_amount, Promo_cd) [stated 2026-10-07 doc:Promotions and Budgets 2026_R2.pptx s.42-50]; settlement happens outside DCODE. Whether this holds on cnr1dev1 (R1): Q-CL4 / Q-CL6 (ON HOLD).

## 8. Rules and validations
All four conditions must hold for a claim to be generated [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.7-10]:
- **C-1 Delivered and N-1:** the orders must be Delivered and their document date must be **N-1**; claims are always generated for N-1 orders, and the same applies to the GIN date. Check on Transaction Inquiry, Document Status [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.8].
- **C-2 Claim Days:** Claim Days must be defined on the distributor profile (Distributor Profile DT, Operative tab) [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.9].
- **C-3 Claim flag:** Promotion Layout Claim = No -> the distributor cannot ask the company for reimbursement; Claim = Yes -> Claimable % is mandatory [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.10].
- **C-4 Resultant type:** Bonus Type 2 (long-term scheme for wholesalers) or Trade Offer (short-term scheme for wholesalers); in Pakistan these currently give customer discounts [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.10]. Matches the BONUS2 / TRADEOFFER lines of Total Offering in the group 11 walks [observed 2026-10-01 G11-1, transaction_inquiry.md].
- Amount rule: Claim % x promotion discount (section 6) [stated 2026-10-08 doc:claim management manual_R2 Aug 2026.pptx s.11].

Schedule and period (three sources, not yet reconciled; contradiction 44, Q-CL1 ON HOLD):
- Promotions deck: a scheduled job runs on the **10th, 20th and 30th**, set per distributor by Claim Days [stated 2026-10-07 doc:Promotions and Budgets 2026_R2.pptx s.42-50].
- Trainer: the claim job is planned **weekly or daily**, per configuration [stated 2026-10-08 Syed Zulfiqar (F8 10-08)].
- BD (CR, QA Verified): was weekly on Saturday night when all DM routes were settled, else Sunday; changed to an **organisation claim calendar** with weekly + month-end dates; on each date the job takes **all delivered orders from the last executed claim's From Date to the current date (To Date), using the actual delivery time**; the route-settlement check was removed for Non-Jupiter claims [jira SDMS-7051] (BD R1, QA Verified) [jira SDMS-7263] [jira SDMS-7265].

Jira rules (to be confirmed live):
- A **Jupiter** claim generated for an **unsettled** open working date is a defect, so Jupiter claims expect the route settled [jira SDMS-12408] (BD R1, Open: describes a bug).
- TH R2 model: claims per IO number for orders delivered and **returns picked** in the period; later returns can give negative claims; no duplicated transactions [jira SDMS-11924] (TH R2).
