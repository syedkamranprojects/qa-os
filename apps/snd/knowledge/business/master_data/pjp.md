## 1. Purpose
- Every **Order Booker** and every **Delivery Man** has a PJP (Permanent Journey Plan), built from **sections** (outlets), **selling category**, **frequency** and **working days**. The Order Booker works only per his PJP; his PJP is downloaded to the handheld as the day's list of outlets to visit [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.4].
- **Selling category** = logical grouping of products (sales, delivery, marketing, promotions). **Section** = group of outlets assigned to a DSR for booking or delivery [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.4].
- Place in the business: master data before the Daily Cycle. The order header's PJP, Section and Selling Category come from it (group 11: PJP 02111-AutomationOB1, Automation_Testing_Section, Selling Category 001) [observed 2026-10-01 G11-1, order_booking.md, test_data/PK.md]. A **PJP creation job** turns the permanent PJP into the daily route records (PJP Daily) used by Route Settlement and the day close [jira SDMS-9885] (PK/BD, cnr1dev1); that job **runs daily** [stated 2026-10-08 Syed Zulfiqar (F8 10-08)].

## 5. Process: the business steps in order
A. Create a PJP [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.5-9]
1. [Maker] Navigate to Distributor Setup > Journey Plan > PJP Creation; Click Add.
2. [Maker] Fill the header (PJP Number, DSR, Working Date, Warehouse, Description ...).
3. [Maker] On Configuration, add rows: Selling Category, Section, Frequency, Repeat Days (and the days), Start Date, Reference PJP (the linked DM / OB PJP).
4. [Maker] Click Save; Click Forward (to the TM user).
5. [Checker] (TM, Auto_Tssm [stated 2026-10-08 Syed Zulfiqar (F7 10-08)]) Open the pending PJP on PJP Creation; Click Forward (approve) or Reject. Approved -> Status Active.

B. Change an approved PJP [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.10-13]
1. [Maker] Header change: PJP Change Request -> edit -> Save -> Forward. Configuration change: Change Track PJP Config -> pick PJP and row -> edit -> Save -> Forward.
2. [Checker] (TM) Forward on the request to approve.

C. Sections and outlets [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.18-23]
1. [Maker] Create the Section (with Default delivery days); map outlets on its Allowable tab, on Distributor Mapping (Section tab), or by Section Bulk Upload (Download Excel, edit, Upload Excel).

D. Daily route [jira SDMS-9885] [stated 2026-10-08 Syed Zulfiqar (F8 10-08)]
1. [System] The PJP creation job (daily) generates the PJP Daily records from the configuration; executed through the Adhoc Executor [jira SDMS-4040] (PKBD, Assigned to QA).

## 6. Outputs and effects
- Approved PJP: Status Active; downloaded to the handheld as the day's outlet list [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.4, s.7].
- The delivery PJP drives delivery: with the delivery app the delivery PJP is mandatory; **without delivery optimisation (DT / non-Locus) a manual picklist is generated from the delivery PJP and the cash memos of the OB PJPs linked to it** [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.8]. (Group 11: the GIN fills DSR, Warehouse, Vehicle from the delivery PJP [observed, glossary DSR].)
- Expected delivery date of an order: Section **Default delivery days** [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.18-19]; the section value **takes precedence over the distributor's default delivery days** [jira SDMS-10310] (R2 hot fix, QA Verified). Whether the planned date comes from this or from the delivery PJP's visit days is Q-PJ2 (ON HOLD); observed bookings carried dates 6 days ahead (10-05 -> 10-11, 10-06 -> 10-12) [observed, BA4].
- PJP Daily records per working date, created by the daily job [jira SDMS-9885]; Order Booker PJP Working Date should follow the daily route generation date [jira SDMS-11041] (R2 story, QA Verified); after Route Settlement with no orders, the Delivery Man PJP Working Date should move to the next scheduled PJP date [jira SDMS-11166] (R2, QA Verified).
- Live 2026-10-09: TM approval makes the PJP **Active** at once (AUTO241009, AUTO241010); an approved PJP Change Request or Change Track PJP Config request is applied at once by the TM's single Forward (description Auto917248; Frequency Daily) [observed 2026-10-09].

## 8. Rules and validations
- The Order Booker works only per his PJP [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.4].
- Every order PJP must be linked to a delivery PJP (Reference PJP) [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.8].
- Only selling categories allowed for the DT are offered on the configuration [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.8].
- PJP Change Request works on approved PJPs only [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.10].
- Section Bulk Upload errors are returned as an error file, message in the last column [stated 2026-10-08 doc:PJP updated R2.pptx s.23].
- Jira (to be confirmed live; PK/BD R1 unless noted):
  - Weekly / Monthly / Yearly without the day(s) is refused with "PJP cannot be scheduled for the weekend marked days." [jira SDMS-6631] (closed as Invalid Bug = treated as expected behaviour).
  - Fortnightly (F2) for PKBD: first visit on the first selected weekday from the start date, then every 14 days until the expiry date [jira SDMS-4040] (Assigned to QA). TH R2 has an outlet-level frequency model F1 / F2 / F4 (visits per month over 24 working days) via "PJP Creation HQ - Outlet level" / PJP Configuration Bulk Upload II; a new configuration's start date sets the previous one's end date to start date - 1 [jira SDMS-10114] (TH R2).
  - The PJP creation job should skip distributor holidays [jira SDMS-9801] (PK prod bug, Assigned to QA) [jira SDMS-10441] (B2B PJPs on DT holidays).
  - GIN approval checks that the PJP configuration covers today; otherwise "PJP not created" (a friendlier message was requested) [jira SDMS-9297] (PK/BD R1, QA In Progress).
